Accuracy

Sun positions are calculated with the NOAA solar position algorithm and checked against the NREL reference (about 0.02°). Real conditions also depend on terrain, buildings, weather, refraction and your phone’s sensors.

Results are guidance, not a survey, an engineering certificate or a legal right-to-light assessment. Check important decisions on site and with a qualified professional.
