Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 October 2026

Summary

Sunlore has no account, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. Your places, skylines and settings stay on your phone.

Location

Location is optional. If you allow it, your position is used on your phone to calculate where the sun is. It is never sent to us.

Searching for a place and showing place names use your phone’s built-in map service (Apple Maps on iPhone, Google on Android). That service receives the search text or the coordinates under its own privacy policy.

Camera and motion sensors

The camera picture is only shown on screen to draw the sun’s path. Nothing is recorded, saved or uploaded. Motion and compass readings are used on the phone only.

Purchases

The one-time Pro purchase is made through the App Store or Google Play; we never see your payment details.

To confirm the purchase, the app uses RevenueCat with an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history. This data is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking.

Data on your phone

Settings, recent and saved places and traced skylines are stored on your phone and are deleted when you delete the app.

Children

Sunlore is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new date. Questions: yusgoktech@gmail.com.
