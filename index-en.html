Sunlore support

Last updated: 4 October 2026

The compass or camera points the wrong way

Move away from metal, cars and magnets, then wave the phone in a figure-8. For the best result, open the Camera tab when the sun is out, put the real sun in the circle and tap “Line up”. The correction is kept for the compass too. Never look straight at the sun.

The sun path in the camera looks stretched

Tap the angle button at the top right of the camera and adjust the field of view until the arc meets the real sun at two different times.

Times look an hour off

Times are always shown in the local time of the selected place, not your phone’s time zone. The Day tab shows the offset in use.

How are “hours of direct sun” calculated?

Every few minutes of each month, the app checks whether the sun is above the skyline you traced with the camera. Garden labels follow a common guide (full sun = 6 h or more); definitions vary between sources.

Restore Pro on a new phone

Open Settings → Sunlore Pro → Restore purchase, signed in with the same store account. Purchases cannot move between iPhone and Android.
